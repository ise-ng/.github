# Security Policy

We take the security and privacy of everyone who uses Ișę seriously. If you've found a vulnerability, thank you — we'd genuinely like to hear from you.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security issues through public GitHub issues, pull requests, or social media.**

Instead, email **[security@getise.com](mailto:security@getise.com)** with:

- A description of the issue and the impact you think it has
- Steps to reproduce it (proof-of-concept welcome)
- Any affected URLs, endpoints, or components you can identify
- Suggested fixes, if you have ideas

If you'd like to encrypt your report, mention it in a first email and we'll share a key.

## What to expect

| Stage | Our target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement that we received your report | Within **2 business days** |
| Initial assessment and severity triage | Within **7 days** |
| Fix or mitigation for confirmed critical issues | As fast as we responsibly can |

We'll keep you updated as we work through it, and we're happy to credit you once a fix is shipped (or keep you anonymous — your call).

## Scope

This policy covers the Ișę platform and its official applications and services. Please act in good faith:

- Don't access, modify, or delete data that isn't yours
- Don't run attacks that degrade service for others (DDoS, spam, brute force)
- Give us a reasonable chance to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly

Acting in good faith under this policy, we won't pursue or support legal action against you for your research.

Thank you for helping keep Ișę safe for everyone doing honest work. 🔒
