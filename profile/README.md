## What we're building

Finding someone trustworthy to fix a tap, move a home, clean an apartment, or run an errand shouldn't be a gamble. Too often, it is.

**Ișę** connects people who need tasks done with verified workers who can do them — with trust built into every step. Real verification, fair pricing, protected payments, and reviews that actually mean something. We're building the layer of trust that Nigeria's everyday economy has been missing.

> Our whole reason for existing: **make honest work easy to find, and easy to trust.**

## How we work

We're a small, senior team that ships. A few things that are true about engineering here:

- **TypeScript end to end** — one language across the stack, strict types, shared validation.
- **A modern, boring-in-the-good-way stack** — NestJS, React, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker.
- **We test what matters** — unit, integration, and end-to-end, run on every change.
- **Automated quality gates** — CI, linting, type-checks and reviews keep `main` always shippable.
- **Clear boundaries** — a clean monorepo today, designed to grow into services tomorrow.
