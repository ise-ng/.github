# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Ișę is built on trust — and that starts with how we treat each other. We pledge to make participation in our community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that build a good community:

- Being kind, patient, and welcoming
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Owning our mistakes and learning from them
- Putting what's best for the community and our users first

Things that don't belong here:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all community spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and email — and any time someone is representing Ișę in public.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to **[conduct@getise.com](mailto:conduct@getise.com)**. All reports are handled confidentially and reviewed promptly. We're committed to protecting people who report in good faith.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, from a warning to a permanent ban, for behavior that violates this code.
